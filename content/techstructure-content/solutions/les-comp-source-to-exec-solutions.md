# Solutions — Source Code, Compilation, and Interpretation

**Lesson ID:** `les-comp-source-to-exec`  
**Scope:** Compare compilation, virtual machines, and interpreted runtimes.

## Worked answers and grading notes

### Solution 1
Python commonly parses and compiles to bytecode executed by a runtime; Go commonly builds a native executable. Actual stages and distribution details depend on implementation, target, and build configuration.

### Solution 2
A Python distribution may need a compatible interpreter and dependencies; a Go executable is target-specific and may still rely on external libraries or OS facilities.
